# Interview Reflection Paragraph

  What surprised us most was finding out our downstream partners need our APIs to populate their database and not just double-check stock at checkout. We expected them to run their own inventory system and occasionally check ours for price comparisons. Seeing that completely changed how we viewed our backend responsibilities. It forced us to rethink our architecture and how we would handle large data syncs. And also for our upstream partners, our websites are quite different therefore we may have to change a few things in our website in order to consume their APIs
